Braving is an independently branded Android browser based on exact Brave releases.

Features:
- Fast and secure Web browsing powered by Chromium
- Powerful tracker and ad-blocking via Brave Shields
- Complete removal of closed-source Google Play dependencies
- Disables crypto wallet modules, advertising rewards, and cloud AI assistants
- Strips telemetry and tracking metrics
- Fully open-source and ready for reproducible F-Droid builds
